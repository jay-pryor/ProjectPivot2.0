# SL-03: Hazards, controls, platforms, and ratings - the record model, links, entered ratings, per-platform IDs
Order: 3 · Risk: high

Acceptance criteria drafted by claude-fable-5-1 at G3 for jay-pryor to accept or rewrite;
they are not human-written until that review is recorded.

## Scope
The record model every later slice builds on: hazards with their causal factors,
consequences, and controls; the control library; platforms with an owner; the links
between them; initial and residual ratings per hazard per platform, shown exactly as
entered and rated on the DEC-002 matrix; the per-platform report ID; and the one
`registry` query every list of a platform's hazards goes through (HZ-004). `rating` is
a pure function of two values and is built here because a residual rating is shown here.

Layers: registry, rating, views (hazard, control, and platform forms and lists).

## Acceptance criteria
1. A hazard is stored once. Linking it to a second platform creates a link and copies
   nothing; editing the hazard's title changes what both platforms show. A hazard may be
   linked to any number of platforms.
2. A hazard holds its causal factors, consequences, preventative controls, and
   mitigating controls, and shows them back as stored.
3. A control is stored once in the control library. One control may be linked to any
   number of hazards, and each of a hazard's controls may be linked separately to each
   platform the hazard is linked to, so the same control can be linked on one platform
   and not on another.
4. Adding a platform leaves every existing hazard, control, reference entry, and link
   equal to what it was, compared record for record.
5. An assessor enters an initial and a residual consequence (1 to 5) and likelihood (A to
   G) for a hazard on each platform it is linked to. The residual values are shown exactly
   as entered, and linking or unlinking a control changes neither.
6. Every rating shown is the DEC-002 cell for its consequence and likelihood, verified
   against a fixture of all 35 cells and the uncategorised case; a rating for a
   consequence or likelihood outside the scales is refused, not shown.
7. Every platform records exactly one user profile as its owner.
8. A hazard linked to a platform receives an ID for reports on that platform; it stays
   the same until a user changes it, and a user can change it.
9. Every list of a platform's hazards includes each hazard linked to it that is neither
   deleted nor retired, with its controls and residual rating, or lists that hazard as
   omitted. All such lists are produced by one registry query, so a hazard cannot be
   absent from one list and present in another.

## Decided at the views CONTRACT session (jay-pryor, 2026-09-15)
- The criteria are entered and read on five new screens, one thing in hand per screen: the
  hazard's own detail, the control library, the platforms, one platform's hazards, and one
  hazard's assessment on one platform (views C-015). Entering ratings, the report ID, and
  the per-platform control links inline on the platform screen was set aside: it makes
  every one of those operations carry a hazard id the screen already knows, and it puts
  criterion 5's two ratings and criterion 3's links on a screen whose job is criterion 9's
  list. Folding a hazard's detail into the hazards screen, and the library and the
  platforms into one screen, was set aside for the same reason.
- Criterion 1's link is made from the platform's screen, not the hazard's: a hazard screen
  that listed the platforms it is linked to would be REQ-026, which is SL-09's. The
  platform screen carries the hazards that could still be linked (views C-019, C-020).
- Criterion 9's "one query" is worded as this module's half: every screen that lists a
  platform's hazards, at this version and later, is built from one
  `registry.listPlatformHazards` and from no other list of hazards, and each omitted hazard
  is named in a message of its own (views C-020). The picker for linking is
  `listHazards` and is explicitly not a list of the platform's hazards.
- Criterion 6's "every rating shown": `views` obtains a band only through
  `rating.ratingFor`, holds no table and no order over bands, and caches none across a
  change (views C-021). `modules/rating/contract` is added to `modules/views/manifest.yaml`,
  which is what makes that mechanical.
- Saving stays on the hazards screen. A change made on any of the five new screens is told
  to `store` at once (views C-012) and shows as unsaved on the hazards screen `back`
  returns to (views C-015); no criterion asks for a save from the other screens, and
  `unsaved` on every screen would have widened every 2.0 screen shape.
