# SL-07: Workflows - the five guided workflows and their immutable records
Order: 7 · Risk: medium

Acceptance criteria drafted by claude-fable-5-1 at G3 for jay-pryor to accept or rewrite;
they are not human-written until that review is recorded.

## Scope
The `workflows` module: add data, onboard a platform, review data, remove a control
from a platform, transfer platform ownership. Each is a guided sequence of steps over
operations the record modules already expose, and each completion writes a record that
cannot be edited afterwards. Two hazards have a clause here besides their primary one:
HZ-002 through REQ-077 and HZ-004 through REQ-008.

Layers: workflows, views (the step screens), calling registry, review-schedule,
change-log, and store through their contracts.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Each of the five workflows is presented as a sequence of named steps, with the current
   step, the steps done, and the steps remaining visible. Recorded as a review row that
   REQ-037 cites, since guidance is expert judgement.
2. Onboarding a platform lets the user select hazards from the existing list; the
   selected hazards' controls and reference-entry links come across without re-entry, and
   each control arrives awaiting confirmation (SL-04), not linked.
3. A review workflow on a platform presents every hazard linked to that platform that is
   neither deleted nor retired, with its controls and residual rating, or lists it as
   omitted; completing it sets the last reviewed date of each record reviewed (SL-06).
4. Completing any workflow records the workflow, the AEST date, the user, and the
   decisions, actions, and outcomes entered. Once complete, no field of that record can
   be changed; the attempt is refused.
5. Removing a control from a platform requires the user, for each hazard on that
   platform the control was linked to, to confirm or re-enter the residual consequence
   and likelihood before the removal is saved.

## Satisfies
`trace/slices.yaml` SL-07: REQ-008, REQ-036, REQ-037, REQ-038, REQ-039, REQ-077.

## Contracts touched
workflows (1.0), views (8.0). Consumes registry, review-schedule, change-log, store unchanged; if a
step needs an operation one of them does not expose, that is an Interface change before
starting.

`workflows` 1.0 is written: the five kinds, their fixed step lists, the stored record, the
gates, and the completion (C-001 to C-013). No operation of registry, review-schedule, or
change-log was found missing, so none of the three changes; `store` is a layer of the slice
(the save) and not an import of this module, which is a function of the working `DataBody`
like every other record module (DEC-006). The acts the steps make are made by `workflows`,
not by `views`, and the step is recorded in the same call — DEC-016's argument one layer up,
recorded as DEC-025. What each `completeReview` and each workflow-record entry is told about
the platforms it reaches is DEC-026.

`views` 8.0 completes the slice's contracts: the register of workflows and one workflow's own
screen, with the four acts of a running workflow (C-036, C-037, C-038). Criterion 1's "visible"
half is that version's and a demonstration row; REQ-037 stays `demonstration` with a `REV-nnn`,
as REQ-057 is, and the row is this slice's to record at acceptance. No requirement is newly
allocated to `views`: all six of this slice's are `workflows`', and what 8.0 adds is the screen
each is read, entered, and refused on. Criterion 3's presentation is `views` C-020's one
`registry.listPlatformHazards` with C-021's band and C-024's controls, which is the same query
the assessment screen and a report are built from, so a review cannot show a different set
(HZ-004). REQ-011 is kept across the new surface rather than re-allocated: C-027 gains the five
changing workflow operations, and 8.0 states outright the invariant it has kept since 5.0 — an
operation is gated exactly when the entry its act appends carries two or more platform ids.

Two things `views` 8.0 decided are recorded rather than passed over, both in DEC-027. Where a
workflow is started and reached again: a register of workflows off the hazards screen, listing
`workflows.listWorkflows` whole, filtered by nothing, as C-028 shows the whole history — the
rejected reading is no list at all, holding the running workflow in the app, which would leave a
workflow saved and loaded again unreachable and make DEC-025's reason for storing it early
unusable until SL-11. The dashboard's in-progress list stays REQ-049's at SL-11 and is out of
scope below. And whether a workflow operation is gated: all five, on
`registry.platformsAffected` of the workflow's subject, which is the same ref DEC-026 gives the
entry each appends — the rejected reading is gating `submitStep` alone, the only one REQ-011's
words reach, which would let an `advanceStep`'s entry land in a queue the user was never warned
about. In fact the gate can fire only on an `add-data` workflow whose hazard has been linked to
two or more platforms since the run began.

Three readings are recorded rather than resolved silently, each with the clause that changes if
the other is the intended one. **What "add data" adds**: one hazard and what belongs to a hazard
(`workflows` C-013), not a chooser over every record kind, which would make the workflow's steps
a tree rather than a sequence. This is the one thing in the module that no criterion here holds
up, and these criteria are still a model's draft for jay-pryor to accept or rewrite. **What
"each record reviewed" is** in criterion 3: the platform and each hazard a person entered a
decision about (C-006), not also each control shown under those hazards, which `SCHEDULABLE_KINDS`
would allow and which would set a date from a decision nobody made (DEC-026). **What the
partial-failure lens's "leave the records as they were" means**: abandoning writes nothing
further and every act already made stands, each already in the history (C-012) — a rollback is
not reachable through any contract of this project, since a hazard's global ID is assigned once
(registry C-002) and a deletion keeps its row (registry C-005). What the lens has left to find is
C-009: a completion makes several acts and either all of them are made or none is.

`workflows` 2.0 (2026-09-17, DEC-038) settles three readings the 1.0 conformance suite could not
test. A removal excludes from the hazards that carry the control as confirmed when it completes,
and it refuses to complete while any of them has no settled residual. A step that takes one
record records once. `describe-the-hazard` refuses no repeat. Criterion 5 is unchanged; its
"for each hazard on that platform the control was linked to" is read at completion.

Criterion 5's requirement is scoped to the workflow, as REQ-077's words are. `views` 4.0 C-025
already offers `excludeControlFromPlatform` on the assessment screen with no ratings gate, and
that path stays: HZ-002's mitigation is registry C-017, which keeps a residual exactly what the
assessor entered, and a direct exclusion changes no rating. Whether the direct path should also
be gated was left as a question for views 8.0 so it would not be discovered as a gap; 8.0
answers it by leaving C-025 exactly as it was and recording it in that contract's not-promised
section. So a user who takes a control off a platform from the assessment screen is not asked to
confirm any hazard's residual, and a user who does it through the workflow is. Gating the direct
path would be an Interface change to C-025 and a change to REQ-077's statement, which is a GATE
decision and not a CONTRACT session's (P10).

## Lenses to run
verification, specification, partial-failure (a workflow abandoned mid-way must leave
the records as they were).

## Out of scope
The dashboard's list of in-progress workflows (SL-11) — `views` C-036 lists every live workflow
of the body, filtered by neither profile nor state, and REQ-049's filtered list is SL-11's. Any
workflow beyond the five named. Correcting a step, going back a step, and any undo of what a
workflow did (`workflows` C-011, C-012). A review tempo on a workflow record: `workflow-record`
is outside `SCHEDULABLE_KINDS` and this slice does not widen it (`workflows` section 7).
