# SL-05: Change log and cross-platform visibility - history of every change and deletion, acknowledgement by affected owners, retirement
Order: 5 · Risk: high

Acceptance criteria drafted by claude-fable-5-1 at G3 for jay-pryor to accept or rewrite;
they are not human-written until that review is recorded.

## Scope
HZ-005 (1D, Serious) and HZ-006 (1C, High), behind `change-log`. Every create, edit, and
deletion of any record or link writes a history entry any user can read; an edit that
reaches more than one platform is shown to the user before saving and to each other
affected platform's owner until acknowledged, keyed by platform so a transfer of
ownership carries the queue. Retirement lives here because a retired hazard leaves
lists, and REQ-076 is what stops that happening while it is still linked.

Layers: change-log, registry (its writes go through change-log; retirement), views (the
history view, the before-save summary, the acknowledgement queue).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Deleting any stored record or link keeps an entry that any user can view, holding
   who deleted it, the AEST time, and what it contained.
2. Creating or editing a hazard, control, platform, reference entry, causal factor,
   consequence, justification, rating, report, or workflow record keeps an entry
   holding who did it, when, and the previous and new values of each changed field.
   For record kinds not yet built, the contract clause is written now and the test
   covers the kinds SL-03 stores.
3. Before saving an edit that changes the control links of more than one platform, or
   the stored content of a hazard, control, or justification linked to more than one
   platform, Pivot lists every platform the edit changes and requires the user to
   proceed past that list.
4. After such an edit is saved, each other affected platform's owner sees the change
   and every item it affects until that owner acknowledges it; acknowledging removes it
   from that owner's queue and from nobody else's.
5. When a platform's owner is changed, every change awaiting the outgoing owner's
   acknowledgement for that platform is shown to the incoming owner until acknowledged.
6. A hazard, control, platform, or reference entry can be marked retired. A hazard
   linked to any platform cannot be retired; the attempt is refused with the platforms
   named.

## Contracts touched
change-log 1.0, registry 4.0, and views 5.0, all written 2026-09-16. `change-log` sits below
`registry` and is keyed by platform (DEC-004); the acknowledgement queue needs no owner from
`registry` — `views` reads `Platform.ownerProfileId` itself and passes only a `PlatformId` to
the log (views C-029) — so the DEC-004 cycle is not reached and this slice does not stop for a
BASELINE session.

## Decided at the change-log CONTRACT session (2026-09-16, DEC-015)
- An entry is one **act** — one call, one profile, one instant — carrying `items`, one per
  record the act changed. An exclusion writes a justification and supersedes a
  `control-platform` link in one call (registry C-021), so one act already changes two
  records, and criterion 4's "the change and every item it affects" is what an entry holds
  and what is acknowledged. One entry per record would let a user acknowledge half an act.
- Each item's `action` — `created`, `edited`, `deleted`, `retired` — is derived from the
  record's status transition and is never given by the caller (C-005), so a caller cannot
  record a deletion as an edit and leave a history that reads as complete (HZ-005).
- `fields` holds each changed field's previous and new value for an `edited` item, and
  every field of the record for a `created`, `deleted`, or `retired` one, which is criterion
  1's "what it contained" (C-002, C-004). `updatedBy` and `updatedAtAest` are excluded: the
  entry's own `createdBy` and `createdAtAest` are the same fact.
- The module reads a record as a baseline `StoredRecord` and knows no kind, so criterion 2's
  "record kinds not yet built" need no change to this contract when they arrive.
- `madeForPlatformId` and `affectedPlatformIds` come from the caller. The platforms an act
  reaches are the live links, which are `registry`'s; a `change-log` that asked would close
  the DEC-004 cycle. Criterion 4's queue is therefore entries whose `affectedPlatformIds`
  hold the platform and whose `madeForPlatformId` does not (C-010).
- Criterion 5 falls out with no data of its own: `listAwaiting` is a function of the body and
  a `PlatformId`, no entry names an owner, and a change of `Platform.ownerProfileId` touches
  nothing here (C-012).
- An acknowledgement is an entry of its own rather than a field of the entry it clears, so
  no row of the collection is ever altered and the operation that clears a queue cannot
  rewrite the history HZ-005 depends on (C-006, C-011).
- Criterion 4's other reading — every item **of that platform** the changed records appear
  in, the hazard's row and its controls — is a composition of `registry`'s per-platform query
  with these entries. It is `views`' half of REQ-012, not refused but placed there; this
  module holds no link and cannot make it.
- Criterion 3 (the before-save list of platforms) and criterion 6 (retirement and its
  refusal) are not this module's: the first is `views` over `registry`'s links, the second is
  `registry`. `change-log` records a retirement as an `ItemAction` and nothing more.
- Allocated here: REQ-010, REQ-012, REQ-045, REQ-081. REQ-011, REQ-067, and REQ-076 stay
  TBD until the `views` and `registry` sessions claim them.

## Decided at the registry CONTRACT session (2026-09-16, DEC-016)
- `registry` imports `modules/change-log/contract` and records its own acts: every changing
  operation appends the entry to the body it returns, and a rejection from the log rejects the
  operation with nothing written (C-023). A consumer that called the log itself could forget
  to, which is HZ-005 with no diff to show it.
- Every changing operation's second argument becomes an `Act` — `{ profile,
  madeForPlatformId }` — in place of the bare `ActiveProfile`. `madeForPlatformId` is required
  and never defaulted: it decides whose queue the change lands in (change-log C-010). This
  changes every changing signature on the surface, so `views` 4.0 is rewritten against it
  before SL-05 is implemented.
- Criterion 3's platforms and criterion 4's queue are one derivation, `platformsAffected`
  (C-024), so what a user is warned about before saving and what then awaits acknowledgement
  cannot differ. REQ-011 stays `views`', over this query.
- Criterion 6's retirement is `retireHazard`, `retireControl`, `retirePlatform` (C-025).
  REQ-067's fourth kind, the reference register's entry, is SL-10's module and has no home
  yet; REQ-067 is allocated to `registry` now and the split is a GATE decision at SL-10.
- Criterion 6 says nothing about the platforms a retired control is already on, which admits
  two readings. Retiring takes a control out of the library only and never off a platform
  (C-027): the other reading makes a confirmed control vanish from a platform's row with
  nobody told, which is HZ-004's shape and the converse of HZ-001. A control leaves a platform
  by an exclusion with a reason (C-020), which is the act SL-04 built.
- REQ-076's refusal names the platforms (C-026). This version has no operation that unlinks a
  hazard from a platform, so a hazard on one cannot be retired at all until a later version
  adds one.
- A change that would write what is already stored rejects with `NoChangeError` (C-029):
  otherwise two calls in the same second hand `change-log` a `before` deep-equal to its
  `after`, which it refuses, and an entry that records nothing is worse than a refusal.
- `setPlatformOwner` (C-028) is what criterion 5 needs from this module; the queue carrying
  across is change-log C-012 and needs no data of its own.

## Decided at the views CONTRACT session (2026-09-16, DEC-017)
- Criterion 3's "before saving" names two moments — before the edit enters the working body,
  and before `save` writes the folder — and the act, its entry, and its queue all happen at
  the first. The list is therefore shown **before the edit is made**: the five operations that
  can reach more than one platform (`renameHazard`, `addCausalFactor`, `addConsequence`,
  `linkControlToHazard`, `retireControl`) resolve with a confirm-edit screen and change
  nothing until `confirmEdit` (C-027). The other reading — one aggregate list at `save` —
  would gate nothing that could still be stopped, since declining would need an undo this
  version does not have.
- The platforms listed are `registry.platformsAffected`'s, the same call whose result
  `registry` C-023 puts on the entry, so criterion 3's list and criterion 4's queue cannot
  differ. None of the five changes a `hazard-platform` link, which is why the list before the
  act and the list on the entry are the same list.
- One platform or none is not a gate. Every other changing operation of this surface reaches
  one platform or none by registry C-024, and `retireHazard` reaches none because a hazard on
  a platform cannot be retired at all (registry C-026).
- `madeForPlatformId` is the platform the screen holds — the platform and assessment screens'
  — and null on every screen that holds no platform, the hazard screen included, because a
  hazard's own screen is platform-independent (C-026). So an edit made there awaits every
  platform it reaches, and an edit made while reading one platform does not await that
  platform's own owner.
- Criterion 4's "every item it affects" is the entry's own items, `change-log`'s reading from
  DEC-015. The other reading `change-log` placed here — the hazard's row and its controls on
  that platform — is not built at 5.0: the entry already holds every record the act changed
  with each field's previous and new value, and the composition is a list view of the kind
  REQ-026 and SL-11 own.
- The queue is every platform the active profile owns, one screen, one `listAwaiting` per
  owned platform; `acknowledgeChange` is offered only for a row the screen shows, which is the
  entitlement half `change-log` left to its consumer (C-029). A retired platform has no queue
  on it, because `registry` 4.0 lists live platforms only; its entries stay awaiting and are
  shown to nobody until a later version lists it. Recorded so it is not discovered as a gap.
- Criteria 1 and 2 are one screen of the whole log (`listEntries`), not a history per record:
  a second entry point into a history screen would make `back` ambiguous, and the navigation
  model holds one edge per screen kind (C-015). `change-log.listHistory` is called at no
  version yet.
- Criterion 6's three retirements are `retireHazard` on the hazard screen, which leaves it,
  and `retireControl` and `retirePlatform` on their lists; the refusal names the platforms by
  name where the live list has one and by id otherwise, which is all a retired platform can be
  named by (C-030). A retired control stays in every hazard's controls and in the state it was
  in on every platform, which is registry C-027 shown.
- Allocated here: REQ-011. REQ-010, REQ-012, REQ-045, and REQ-081 stay `change-log`'s and
  REQ-067 and REQ-076 stay `registry`'s; this version adds the screens they are read on, cited
  in the clause that shows each, and claims none of them.
