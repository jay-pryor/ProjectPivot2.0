# SL-04: Control inheritance and confirmation - what a platform shows of its hazards' controls, and who confirmed each
Order: 4 · Risk: high

Acceptance criteria drafted by claude-fable-5-1 at G3 for jay-pryor to accept or rewrite;
they are not human-written until that review is recorded.

## Scope
The two hazards HZ-001 (1D, Serious) and HZ-007 (1C, High), both behind `registry`. A
platform shows a control as its own only when a stored link exists; every control of a
linked hazard that the platform does not carry is shown with its justification; a link
to a platform exists only after a person confirmed it, and the confirmation says who and
when. Small on purpose, so the two catastrophic-consequence hazards get their own lens
run and targeted read.

Layers: registry, views (the platform's control view and the confirmation step).

## Acceptance criteria
1. A control is shown as linked to a platform only when a stored link between that
   control and that platform exists. Deleting the link removes the control from the
   platform's view; nothing else does.
2. For each hazard linked to a platform, every control of that hazard that is not linked
   to the platform is shown against the platform, with the justification recorded for
   leaving it unlinked.
3. When a user leaves one of a hazard's controls unlinked from a platform the hazard is
   linked to, Pivot requires a justification and does not save the state without one.
4. A control is not linked to a platform until a user confirms it for that platform. The
   link, once made, records the confirming profile and the AEST time. A control awaiting
   confirmation is visible as such and is not shown as linked.

## Decided at the registry CONTRACT session (2026-09-15, DEC-014)
- The four criteria are three states of one (hazard, control, platform) triple: `confirmed`
  (a live `control-platform` link), `excluded` (a live `justification` record with the text
  a person wrote), `awaiting` (neither). Registry 3.0 promises at most one of the two rows
  per triple and never both (C-021).
- Criteria 2 and 4 are read together: every one of a hazard's controls is shown against the
  platform whatever its state; an excluded one carries its reason; one nobody has ruled on
  is `awaiting`, not "unlinked without a justification". Criterion 3's requirement of a
  reason bites on the act of ruling a control off, not on the passage of time before anyone
  rules. The other reading — no save while any control is unlinked without a reason — makes
  criterion 4's awaiting state unreachable and is refused in registry 3.0's version note.
- Criterion 4's "records the confirming profile and the AEST time" is the confirming link's
  `createdBy` and `createdAtAest` (C-022), not a second pair of fields; the
  `control-platform` link gains no field, so nothing stored at 2.0 changes shape.
- 2.0's `linkControlToPlatform` and `unlinkControlFromPlatform` are replaced by
  `confirmControlForPlatform` and `excludeControlFromPlatform`, the latter carrying the
  reason: REQ-058's forbidden state is then unwritable rather than guarded by the screen.
  `views` 3.0 C-024 is the one consumer and is rewritten by views' SL-04 CONTRACT session,
  which must run before SL-03 is implemented, because SL-03's unlink is now this exclusion.
- REQ-001, REQ-013, REQ-058, and REQ-070 are allocated to `registry`. REQ-002 stays with
  `views`, as REQ-009 does: registry supplies every control with its state and reason in
  `listPlatformHazards` (C-019), and which of them a screen shows is views' to promise.
- Bulk confirmation is explicitly not promised: each act names one control and one profile,
  so SL-07's onboarding is built from these acts rather than from a wider one (HZ-007).

## Decided at the views CONTRACT session (2026-09-16, views 4.0)
- `views` 4.0 C-024 shows every one of a hazard's controls against the platform in the one
  state registry C-021 gives it, on both screens that show a control against a platform: the
  assessment screen's `controls` and each row of the platform screen. A control is shown as the
  platform's only while its state is `confirmed` (criterion 1), an `excluded` one carries the
  stored reason (criterion 2), and an `awaiting` one is shown as awaiting rather than as
  unlinked (criterion 4). The module derives no state and counts controls nowhere.
- C-025 is the two acts, `confirmControlForPlatform(app, controlId)` and
  `excludeControlFromPlatform(app, controlId, text)`, each naming one control and neither
  taking a list or a default (HZ-007). They replace 3.0's `linkControlToPlatform` and
  `unlinkControlFromPlatform`, which is the rewrite DEC-014 said this session would make;
  SL-03's unlink is now this exclusion, so SL-03's IMPLEMENT builds against 4.0.
- Criterion 3 is kept by the exclusion carrying the text: a blank reason is refused by
  `registry`, `store.noteChange` is not called, and nothing is stored, so no screen of this
  module ever shows a control excluded with no reason.
- Criterion 4's "records the confirming profile and the AEST time" is read on the assessment
  screen, which gains `profiles` — every stored profile, one `profiles.listProfiles` per build
  — so a confirmation's `byProfileId` is drawn as a name, as a platform's owner already is
  (C-018) and as a superseding save's profile is (C-007). The platform screen's rows carry the
  `Confirmation` as `registry` gave it and no name; resolving one there is not promised.
- A `profiles.listProfiles` that fails mid-session on the platforms or assessment screen
  leaves the screen with `profiles` empty and a warning naming the file, the working body
  kept — not the folder screen, which would discard unsaved work. 3.0 left this condition
  unstated on the platforms screen; section 4 now carries one row for both screens.
