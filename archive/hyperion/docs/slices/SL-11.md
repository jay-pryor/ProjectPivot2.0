# SL-11: Dashboard, open items, and filters
Order: 11 · Risk: low

Acceptance criteria drafted by claude-fable-5-1 at G3; the readings below were ruled by
jay-pryor in the SL-11 views CONTRACT session on 2026-09-16 and written into the criteria.

## Scope
The last of `views`: the per-user dashboard, the open-items view that shows everything
whatever its owner, the flag on unlinked records, and filtering of hazards and controls.
Most items shown here are produced by queries earlier slices built; the status filter and
the unlinked flag need `registry` reads of records and links whatever their status, which
no earlier slice exposed (see *Contracts touched*).

Layers: views, calling registry, rating, review-schedule, change-log, workflows,
reference-register.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Hazards and controls can be filtered by platform, by risk rating, by status, and by
   reference entry, singly and in combination; the filtered list is a subset of the
   unfiltered one and every item in it satisfies every active filter.
   - Risk rating is a hazard's residual band on the platform it is listed against, and a
     control's is the residual band of the hazard row it appears on.
   - Status is both the record's own status (live, retired, deleted) and a control's
     state on a platform (confirmed, excluded, awaiting); either may be filtered on. A
     hazard has no control state, so while a control-state filter is active the hazards
     list is empty and only the controls list is narrowed.
   - A reference-entry filter matches only the hazards and controls that entry links to
     directly, not those reached through a hazard or a platform it links to.
2. A user's dashboard shows the overdue reviews and unconfirmed controls on the
   platforms that user owns, the workflows that user has in progress, and the changes
   awaiting that user's acknowledgement, and nothing belonging to another user.
   - Overdue reviews are those of each owned platform, of the hazards on it, of the
     controls on those hazards' rows, and of the reference entries linked to any of them.
   - An unconfirmed control is one in the `awaiting` state on an owned platform; an
     `excluded` control is not unconfirmed.
   - The workflows are those in progress whose subject is an owned platform.
3. The open-items view shows every open item of every kind named in REQ-049 and REQ-068,
   whatever its owner, in a view separate from the dashboard.
4. Every hazard, control, platform, or reference entry that is linked to nothing and is
   not retired is flagged as an open item; linking it, or retiring it, clears the flag.
   - Only live records are flagged; a deleted record is not an open item.
   - A link is a hazard-control link, a hazard-platform link, a reference entry's link, a
     hazard's causal factor or consequence, or a workflow in progress whose subject is the
     record.
   - A link counts whatever the status of the record at its other end: a control linked
     only to a retired or deleted hazard is linked, and is not flagged.

## Contracts touched
views, and registry. Criterion 1's record-status filter needs hazards, controls, and
platforms listed whatever their status, and criterion 4's link rule needs a control's and
a platform's links whatever the status of the hazard at the other end; registry exposes
neither, so an SL-11 registry CONTRACT session runs before views 11.0 is written.

That session took registry to 5.0 (DEC-036). `listAllHazards`, `listAllControls`, and
`listAllPlatforms` give every record whatever its status (C-030). `listLinks` gives every live
link that names a hazard, control, or platform, whatever the records at either end are (C-031).
C-010 states that `getHazardDetail` resolves for a hazard of any status. Which of those links,
and which causal factors and consequences, make a record linked stays views', as criterion 4
rules. No changing operation, stored shape, or import of registry changed.

views 11.0 composes them: the filter screen (C-045), the dashboard (C-046), the open-items screen
(C-047), and the unlinked flag on it (C-048), with the shapes DEC-037 records.
