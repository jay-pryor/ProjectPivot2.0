# DEC-038: A removal excludes from the hazards confirmed when it completes, a one-record step records once, and describing a hazard refuses no repeat
Date: 2026-09-17
Status: proposed
Tier: interface

## Context
The CONFORMANCE session for `workflows` 1.0 left three things untested because the contract
could be read two ways on each, and a suite cannot hold a promise the contract does not make.
First, what a `remove-control` completion excludes from: C-007's "each of those hazards" names
the rows carrying the control as confirmed, but the `completeWorkflow` doc in `contract.js`
said "each hazard settled". Second, what C-004's "at most one entry per record it names" refuses
at a step that takes one record, when a second submission names a different one. Third, whether
`describe-the-hazard` refuses a repeat, when each of its submissions creates a new record.
jay-pryor ruled the first, relayed by that session: the hazards confirmed at the moment of
completion. The other two were proposed by that session. Deferring them leaves the suite with
three holes and the implementation free to pick either reading.

## Decision
`workflows` 2.0. At completion, a removal reads the hazards carrying the chosen control as
confirmed on the platform from the body of that call, as every C-008 demand already is. It
excludes from those hazards only. It rejects with `StepIncompleteError` for
`settle-the-ratings`, naming them, while any has no settlement, so no control comes off a hazard
whose residual nobody settled (REQ-077). A settled hazard no longer in that set is not excluded
from, and its settlement entry stands. An empty set completes with no exclusion. At
`record-the-outcome`, `stepDemand`'s `outstanding` is exactly those unsettled refs. The five
steps that take one record refuse any second submission with `AlreadyRecordedError`, carrying
the ref of the entry already recorded. `describe-the-hazard` refuses nothing as a repeat.

## Alternatives considered
### Exclude from the hazards settled at `settle-the-ratings`
Rejected because: it acts on a list kept from an earlier step, which C-008 rules out for every
demand. It would call `excludeControlFromPlatform` for a hazard whose control was excluded in
the meantime, and then reject the whole completion with `AlreadyExcludedError` for a state the
user wanted anyway. It would also leave on the platform a control confirmed for a new hazard
after the step, while the completed record says the control was removed.

### At completion, exclude from an unsettled hazard as well
Rejected because: that takes a control off a hazard whose residual nobody confirmed or
re-entered, which is exactly what REQ-077 and HZ-002 exist to stop.

### Refuse only a repeat of the same record at a one-record step
Rejected because: a second `name-the-hazard` naming a new title would create a second hazard and
move `subject`, and a second `choose-the-control` would change which control the settlements
are about. C-008 already says these steps are satisfied once their one entry is recorded.

### Refuse a `describe-the-hazard` submission with the same part and text
Rejected because: no stored text is unique anywhere in this project (registry's not-promised
section), and two identical causal factors are a user's choice to correct in `registry`, not a
repeat of one record.

## Consequences
- Makes easy: every C-007 and C-008 test reads one set from one body. C-009 is reached by an
  unreadable history or schedule entry rather than by an exclusion.
- Makes hard: a removal that has a hazard confirmed mid-run cannot complete, and because steps
  do not go back (C-002), it is abandoned and started again. `views` has no section 4 row yet for
  `StepIncompleteError` from `completeWorkflow`.
- Locks in: acts made at completion follow the body at completion, not the steps.

## Reversal
Cost to undo: hours while G3 has not passed. No stored field changes, and only the suite and
`views` read these clauses.
Would be triggered by: hands-on use finding the abandon-and-restart path too harsh, which would
argue for a way back to `settle-the-ratings` rather than for excluding unsettled hazards.
