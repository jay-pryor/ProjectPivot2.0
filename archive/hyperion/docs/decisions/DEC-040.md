# DEC-040: HZ-009 is mitigated at reports C-006 and claimed by SL-09, not SL-06
Date: 2026-09-23
Status: accepted
Tier: internal

## Context
HZ-009 says Pivot must never produce a report from out-of-date data without telling the user.
At SL-06 the `review-schedule` CONTRACT session pointed its `mitigation_contract` at
review-schedule C-009, the earliest checkable clause, with `mitigation_test: TBD` and
`mitigation_status: proposed`, and recorded in its section 7 that the hazard "is not verifiable
at this version": C-009 gives an overdue flag against a fixed clock and nothing that warns before
a report. It also recorded that SL-06's claim on HZ-009 in `trace/slices.yaml` would block that
slice's acceptance, since an accepted slice may claim only verified hazards (CORE-TRC-002), and
left the move to a GATE session (P10). `docs/slices/SL-06.md` carries the same note.

At SL-09 the `reports` CONTRACT session wrote C-006: `produceReport` refuses unless it is handed
exactly the out-of-date list `outOfDateFor` gives at the moment of the call, and the report stores
that list (DEC-034). Its section 7 records C-006 as the hazard's telling, and again leaves moving
the mitigation and the slice claim to a GATE decision. G3 cannot pass while a hazard's mitigation
points at a clause that cannot verify it and a test that does not exist (CORE-LFC-005 exit
criteria), so the decision is taken now.

## Decision
HZ-009's `mitigation_contract` is `modules/reports/CONTRACT.md::C-006`. Its `mitigation_test` is
`modules/reports/conformance/test_invariants.test.js::C-006, HZ-009: a record is out of date from
the day after its due date at any hour, not on the due date at any hour, and outOfDateFor always
equals listOverdue over what the report uses (REQ-016; SL-09 criterion 3)`, which holds the
never-statement over random bodies and clocks. Two further reports cases encode the same clause
and are support, not the named test: `test_operations.test.js::C-006: a report produced with the
list acknowledged exactly carries that list as outOfDate, ...` and `test_errors.test.js::section
4 row 16: a list shown and then changed ... is refused with the list as it now is, ...`. The
screen half, that the list is shown to the user before producing, is views C-042
(`test_operations.outputs.test.js`, `test_errors.outputs.test.js`) and is support too.
`mitigation_status` moves from `proposed` to `traced`; it reaches `verified` when the named test
passes, at SL-09.

HZ-009 leaves SL-06's `hazards` in `trace/slices.yaml` and joins SL-09's. SL-06 keeps HZ-012 and
stays unblocked for acceptance. Review-schedule C-006 and C-009 remain what the telling is built
from, and stay cited in HZ-009's requirements (REQ-015, REQ-021 by `source:`); nothing about
review-schedule's contract changes.

## Alternatives considered
### Keep the mitigation at review-schedule C-009 and let HZ-009 stay `proposed` until SL-09
Rejected because: a `proposed` hazard is an error once G3 is recorded as passed (CORE-TRC-001), so
G3 could not pass; and C-009 cannot verify the never-statement at any version, because the
telling is not in that module (its own section 7 says so).

### Name the operations test, or the errors test, as the mitigation test
Rejected because: the operations case checks one body with the list acknowledged and one with
nothing due, and the errors case checks the refusal when the list changes; the invariants case
holds the property over random bodies and clocks and covers the day-after boundary that HZ-009's
"out of date" turns on. The other two are cited as support so a reviewer finds them.

### Split HZ-009 into a flag hazard at SL-06 and a telling hazard at SL-09
Rejected because: the never-statement is one statement about a report, and the flag alone
harms nobody; a second hazard record would be HZ-012's territory (an overdue review unflagged)
restated.

## Consequences
- Makes easy: SL-06's acceptance record, which now claims only HZ-012; the G3 exit criterion
  that every hazard mitigation names a contract clause and a test.
- Makes hard: nothing. review-schedule's contract, suite, and section 7 are unchanged; the
  section 7 note there now reads as history and is corrected at that module's next CONTRACT
  session, as reports' section 7 is at its own.
- Locks in: reports C-006 as the one clause a change to HZ-009's mitigation would have to touch.

## Reversal
Cost to undo: minutes. Three fields in `trace/hazards.yaml`, two lists in `trace/slices.yaml`,
and the two slice definitions' Satisfies lines.
Would be triggered by: a ruling that the warning before a report belongs to `views` alone
(DEC-034 rejected it), which would move the clause to views C-042; or reports gaining reference
entries, which changes what the test covers but not where the mitigation sits.
