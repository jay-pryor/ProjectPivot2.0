# DEC-002: Platform hazards are rated on the company risk matrix, fixed in Pivot
Date: 2026-09-14
Status: accepted
Tier: baseline

## Context
REQ-017 requires every risk rating Pivot shows to equal the matrix rating for its
consequence and likelihood, and cited DEC-001 for the matrix. DEC-001 is a decision about
how Pivot's own hazard records in `trace/` are encoded; it is superseded when that
encoding changes, which would leave a product requirement pointing at a dead record. The
product needs its own decision naming the matrix platform hazards are rated on.

## Decision
Pivot rates every hazard on every platform on the company risk matrix reproduced below,
the same matrix DEC-001 scores Pivot's own hazards on. The matrix is fixed in Pivot, not
user-editable data. Pivot stores and shows a consequence as its level number (1 to 5) and
a likelihood as its letter (A to G), and shows a rating as the matrix cell, for example
`2C = Serious`. The integer likelihood encoding in DEC-001 applies to `trace/` records
only and never appears in Pivot's stored data or on screen.

### Likelihood values

| Level | Specific Individual Item | Fleet or Inventory | FNC Fleet Failures | Fleet Failure Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Frequent (A)** | Likely to occur often in the life of an item. | Continuously experienced. | >10 | More than 10 instances over fleet life. Occurs at least annually across the fleet. |
| **Probable (B)** | Will occur several times in the life of an item. | Will occur frequently. | 10 | Between 5 and 10 instances over fleet life. |
| **Occasional (C)** | Likely to occur sometime in the life of an item. | Will occur several times. | 5 | Between 1 and 5 instances over the fleet life. |
| **Remote (D)** | Unlikely, but possible to occur in the life of an item. | Unlikely, but can reasonably be expected to occur. | 1 | No more than one instance over the fleet life. |
| **Improbable (E)** | So unlikely, it can be assumed occurrence may not be experienced in the life of an item. | Unlikely to occur, but possible. | 0.1 | Less than a 0.1 probability of instance over the fleet life. |
| **Eliminated (F)** | Incapable of occurrence. This level is used when potential hazards are identified and later eliminated. | Incapable of occurrence. This level is used when potential hazards are identified and later eliminated. | 0 | Zero instances over the fleet life. |

### Consequence values

| Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Catastrophic (1)** | Could result in one or more of the following: death, permanent total disability, irreversible significant environmental impact, or monetary loss equal to or exceeding $10M. |
| **Critical (2)** | Could result in one or more of the following: permanent partial disability, injuries or occupational illness that may result in hospitalisation of at least three personnel, reversible significant environmental impact, or monetary loss equal to or exceeding $1M but less than $10M. |
| **Marginal (3)** | Could result in one or more of the following: injury or occupational illness resulting in one or more lost work day(s), reversible moderate environmental impact, or monetary loss equal to or exceeding $100K but less than $1M. |
| **Negligible (4)** | Could result in one or more of the following: injury or occupational illness not resulting in a lost work day, minimal environmental impact, or monetary loss less than $100K. |
| **None (5)** | No credible accident / mishap or harm to either personnel or environment. |

## Alternatives considered
### Keep REQ-017 citing DEC-001
Rejected because: DEC-001 decides the trace-record encoding for Pivot's own hazards and is
superseded on its own triggers; a product requirement would then cite a superseded record.

### Make the matrix user-editable data inside Pivot
Rejected because: two copies of Pivot could then rate the same consequence and likelihood
differently, which is HZ-010 by another route; the company matrix changes rarely and a
change to it is a decision, not a setting.

### Store and show likelihood as the DEC-001 integer (1 to 7)
Rejected because: the integer scale reads backwards to anyone who knows the matrix (1 is
the most likely), so a rating could be read as its mirror image and every report would be
plausibly and consistently wrong.

## Consequences
- Makes easy: reading a Pivot rating straight off the company matrix; a reference
  validation of REQ-017 against a fixture of every matrix cell.
- Makes hard: adopting a different matrix for one platform or one customer.
- Locks in: the cell notation (number then letter) in every stored rating, every report,
  and every bow-tie diagram.

## Reversal
Cost to undo: days (re-rate or re-map every hazard on every platform, regenerate every
stored report).
Would be triggered by: the organisation replacing or renumbering this matrix.
