# DEC-036: Records of every status and the live links that name a record are two reads of their own, and whether a record is "linked" stays the consumer's
Date: 2026-09-16
Status: proposed
Tier: interface

## Context
SL-11 is `views`' and its definition said it touched no record module, but two of its criteria
cannot be built from `registry` 4.0. Criterion 1 filters hazards and controls by status, and
jay-pryor ruled, relayed by the `views` CONTRACT session, that the filter covers a record's own
status (live, retired, deleted) as well as a control's state on a platform. But
`listHazards`, `listControls`, and `listPlatforms` give live records only, and
`listPlatformHazards` leaves out hazards that are not live. Criterion 4 flags records linked to
nothing, and jay-pryor ruled that a control whose only link goes to a retired or deleted hazard
counts as linked. Nothing in 4.0 goes from a control or a platform to its links. Both reads had
to exist before `views` 11.0 could be written against them.

## Decision
`registry` 5.0 adds four reads and changes nothing else. `listAllHazards`, `listAllControls`,
and `listAllPlatforms` give every record of their kind whatever its status, in the order of the
live list, which is exactly their live entries (C-030). `listLinks(body, ref)` gives every live
link that names a hazard, control, or platform, whatever the status of the records at either
end (C-031). C-010 now says that `getHazardDetail` resolves for a hazard of any status, which
4.0 implied and did not state. The rule that decides whether a record is linked stays with
`views`: which link kinds count, and whether a hazard's causal factors and consequences count
too, which jay-pryor has ruled they do.

## Alternatives considered
### A status argument on `listHazards`, `listControls`, and `listPlatforms`
Rejected because: the 1.0 signature of `listHazards` is promised unchanged, and every existing
`views` call site and conformance test reads "no argument" as "live only". An optional argument
defaulting to live would keep them passing, but it would turn a list that cannot show a deleted
record into one that can when given one more value. That is HZ-004's shape in reverse, and it
would be one easy argument away in every caller.

### A boolean `isLinked(body, ref)` in `registry`
Rejected because: REQ-068 is `views`', and what counts as linked is still being ruled on. For
example, causal factors count, and whether a platform linked only to a deleted hazard counts is
not settled. Putting the rule here would make each new ruling an Interface change to a record
module, and it would split one requirement between two contracts. Listing the links is a fact
this module holds, and the ruling on it is not.

### Include superseded links in `listLinks`
Rejected because: section 3 defines "linked" as a live link. A superseded `control-platform`
link is history, which is `change-log`'s (C-023). Listing it would make every consumer filter by
status to recover the meaning the rest of the contract uses.

## Consequences
- Makes easy: a status filter over every record, and a platform filter over a record of any
  status (`listLinks` or `platformsAffected`), both from reads `views` may import.
- Makes hard: nothing already promised. `views` must combine `listLinks` and `getHazardDetail` for
  a hazard's flag itself.
- Locks in: four more reads on the surface, and the rule, as in C-024 and C-027, that a link's
  own status decides.

## Reversal
Cost to undo: hours while G3 has not passed. The reads change nothing stored, and `views` is the
only consumer.
Would be triggered by: a later ruling that puts the "linked" rule into the record module, or a
record kind whose links are not in `collections.link`.
